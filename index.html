Lead story

Bangladeshi student builds the first Bengali programming language

Ojogor (অজগর) is written in Bengali syntax. Its author, in Dhaka, founded Appibrium and posted a telephony preprint on ResearchGate.

By Ratul Hasan Ruhan

Ratul Hasan Ruhan is a software engineer and systems researcher in Dhaka, Bangladesh. He builds technology for Bangla speakers and created Ojogor (অজগর), the first Bengali programming language: an interpreter for writing and running code in Bengali syntax, especially for beginners. The language received the Bangladesh ICT & Innovation Award 2025.

He works across mobile, web, desktop, embedded systems, and core computer science. He started with Android development and expanded into cross-platform engineering, applied AI and machine learning, systems programming, and infrastructure. He is interested in how systems are designed, how components interact, and how research becomes a reliable application.

I value clarity, maintainability, and thoughtful engineering over quick fixes.

Ratul Hasan Ruhan, profile

Since October 2025 he has been Founder and Full-Stack Mobile Architect at Appibrium, designing mobile systems from the interface to the backend, with DevOps and machine learning in the loop. He is studying for a BSc (Hons) in Computing at the University of Greenwich (NCC Pathway).

His preprint on the Caller Collision Resolution Protocol was posted on ResearchGate in October 2025. The DOI is 10.13140/RG.2.2.26499.98081. He is open to research collaboration, MSc and PhD opportunities, and full-time roles.

Science & Research

A preprint on calls that collide

Caller Collision Resolution Protocol (CCRP): Bridging Simultaneous Call Attempts in Real-World Telephony. Posted on ResearchGate in October 2025. This is a preprint, not a journal article.

October 2025 · ResearchGate · DOI 10.13140/RG.2.2.26499.98081

The preprint describes a protocol for mutual call collisions: two parties call each other at the same time and both receive a busy signal. Read it on ResearchGate.
