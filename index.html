Letters to the Editor

Questions already on the desk

Who is Ratul Hasan Ruhan?

Ratul Hasan Ruhan is a software engineer and systems researcher based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. He builds technology for Bangla speakers and is the creator of Ojogor, the first Bengali programming language.

What is Ojogor?

Ojogor (অজগর) is the first Bengali programming language. It enables writing code with Bengali syntax. Ojogor received the Bangladesh ICT & Innovation Award 2025.

What is Ishara?

Ishara is a real-time Bengali sign language translation system focused on accessibility and inclusion. It received the National Tech Award 2025.

What awards has he won?

Recognitions include National AI BUILD-A-THON – Trailblazer (Jan 2026), Coding Fest 2026 – 1st Place, Bangladesh ICT & Innovation Award 2025, Startup World Cup 2025 Regional Finalist, and multiple awards at DIA ICT Fest 2025.

What is his research?

His research includes the Caller Collision Resolution Protocol (CCRP), a preprint published on ResearchGate in Oct 2025 (DOI: 10.13140/RG.2.2.26499.98081).

Where did he study?

He studies BSc (Hons) Computing at the University of Greenwich (NCC Pathway) and previously studied Computer Science & Engineering at Daffodil International University.

Is he open to new opportunities?

Yes — open to research collaboration, MSc/PhD opportunities, and full‑time roles.

How to contact him?

Reach him by email at ratulhasan1644@gmail.com or via LinkedIn at linkedin.com/in/ratul-hasan-ruhan.
