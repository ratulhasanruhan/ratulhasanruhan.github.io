Caller Collision Resolution Protocol (CCRP): Bridging Simultaneous Call Attempts in Real-World Telephony

This paper introduces the Caller Collision Resolution Protocol (CCRP), a novel framework designed to resolve mutual call collisions—a persistent issue in telephony where two parties calling each other simultaneously receive busy signals.
